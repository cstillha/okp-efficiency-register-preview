FRAGESTELLUNG

Worum geht es?

Sind CPAP-Mietpreise, Zubehör und die ambulante Versorgung heute höher vergütet als wirtschaftlich notwendig – oder erklären Bedarf, Mietdauer und Versorgungsumfang die Kosten?

Was wissen wir?

  • Für die CPAP-Miete gilt seit 1. Juli 2026 ein Höchstvergütungsbetrag von CHF 1,36 pro Tag; zuvor waren es CHF 1,50.
  • Die Mietposition umfasst unter anderem Wartung, Wartungsmaterial, Aufbereitung und Rücknahme.
  • Der Höchstvergütungsbetrag ist eine Obergrenze. Für die OKP ist der effektive verrechnete Preis massgebend; aus dem HVB allein folgt kein tatsächlicher Marktpreis.

You receive only one unverified signal. You have no predefined question tree, no prior case conclusion, no goldset and no hidden manual dossier.

First design the investigation questions yourself. Then research them with public web sources inside this single model call.

The investigation must be decision-useful and current to August 2026. Cover, where relevant: expenditure development; P (price/tariff/remuneration) versus Q (quantity/utilisation); access and unmet need; population effects; provider supply; treatment intensity/duration; outcomes and quality; regional variation; incentives; regulatory/system changes; current monitoring/reforms; parliamentary or institutional action; stakeholder positions; plausible counterhypotheses; and decisive data gaps.

Rules:
1. Treat the signal as a lead, not a fact set. Verify it and seek evidence that weakens as well as supports the concern.
2. Prefer official Swiss primary sources for factual/current claims. Use stakeholder primary sources for stakeholder positions and clearly distinguish advocacy from independent evidence.
3. Current-state claims must be current in 2026 where possible. Separate historical evidence from current status.
4. Do not infer overuse from higher utilisation, inefficiency from expenditure growth, or causality from association.
5. Do not confuse a higher tariff level with the share of total expenditure growth attributable to tariffs.
6. Explicitly distinguish price/remuneration effects from quantity/utilisation and case-mix/intensity effects.
7. If the reform was intended to improve access, investigate whether access actually improved and what remains unknown about outcomes and appropriateness.
8. Do not invent calculations. Flag useful deterministic calculations or decompositions as next research actions.
9. For each question state evidence needed, evidence found, remaining gaps and next research actions.
10. Include the materially involved stakeholders and active 2026 processes, but do not treat positions as evidence.
11. Write in German. Every substantive sourced item must carry a real public URL discovered in this run.
12. Be self-critical. The final assessment must say what is established, what is plausible, and what is not demonstrated.
